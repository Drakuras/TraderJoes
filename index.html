About This Program

The Grocery Assistance Partnership is an initiative designed to provide supplemental food support to eligible households facing increased grocery costs. Through partnerships with major retailers like Trader Joe's, we distribute vouchers funded by brand partnerships and consumer participation programs.

Since 2020, we have assisted over 127,000 families nationwide with an average benefit of $650 per household.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cost to participate?

There is no monetary cost. Participants invest approximately 15-20 minutes completing participation tasks (surveys, app trials, or promotional offers).

Who is eligible for this program?

Participants must be 18 years or older and reside in the USA, Canada, UK, or Australia. All household types are eligible.

How long does the process take?

Most participants complete the verification and tasks within 15-20 minutes. Voucher codes are typically delivered within 24 hours of task completion.

How do I receive my voucher?

Upon verification of completed tasks, your digital voucher code will be sent to your provided email address.
